% Titel der Arbeit
\Vtitle{Large Language Models in Human-machine interaction}
% Autor der Arbeit
\Vauthor{Ömer Emre Mutlu}
% e-mail des Autors
\Vaddress{emre.mutlu@rwth-aachen.de}
% Kurzfassung der Arbeit
\Vabstract{
    Large language models have recently emerged as a powerful interface paradigm for human machine interaction, particularly in safety critical domains where speed, reliability, and cognitive support are essential. This work presents a structured literature review of large language model based interaction mechanisms across text based, audio based, and visual language modalities, including visual language models that jointly process visual and linguistic information. Architectural foundations such as retrieval augmented generation, tool calling, agentic reasoning, speech to speech models, and visual language models are examined, followed by representative case studies from aerospace operations, emergency and environmental response, and industrial settings. Across these domains, the findings indicate that hybrid architectures combining large language model based high level reasoning with deterministic low level execution and human in the loop control offer the most reliable and usable solutions. While challenges related to bias, latency, multimodal integration, and robustness remain, the reviewed evidence suggests that carefully constrained and multimodal large language model based interfaces can meaningfully reduce operator workload and enhance decision support in safety critical human machine interaction.
}
